source: "CAPES / PPgSI"
---

The Program Coordinating Committee (CCP) has released the results of the internal selection for the **Doctoral Sandwich Program Abroad (PDSE/CAPES)**.

The selected students will receive financial support to spend a research period at partner institutions abroad, contributing to the internationalization of the program and to the strengthening of research partnerships.

The list of names and the scholarship amounts are posted on the secretariat's bulletin board and were sent by e-mail to the applicants.
