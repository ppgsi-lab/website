PPgSI has opened the internal selection for the distribution of the master's and doctoral **CNPq scholarships** granted to the program for 2025.

## Who can apply

- Students duly enrolled in PPgSI in 2025.
- Exclusive dedication to the program (no employment relationship incompatible with the scholarship).
- Academic transcript and academic output in accordance with CCP criteria.

## Evaluation criteria

1. Academic performance (grades in the courses taken).
2. Scientific output (papers, technical reports).
3. Alignment of the project with the program's research lines.

The forms and the schedule are in [Scholarship rules](/academic/funding).
