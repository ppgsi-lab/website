Papers by PPgSI advisors and advisees were recognized as **best papers** in tracks of the **Brazilian Symposium on Information Systems (SBSI)**, the main event in the field in Brazil, organized by the Brazilian Computer Society (SBC).

The papers covered topics from both of the program's research lines, including empirical software engineering, human-computer interaction, pattern recognition, and IT management.

## Impact

The recognition reinforces the program's high-quality output and PPgSI's integration into the national Information Systems community. The full list of awarded papers and their authors is available in [Scientific output](/about/production).
