---
title: "PPgSI academic mission to German universities"
date: 2026-03-05
summary: "Four faculty advisors from the program make a technical visit to partner universities in Germany to strengthen research collaborations."
source: "PPgSI"
---

Four PPgSI faculty advisors took part in an academic mission to German universities, with the aim of strengthening research collaborations, exploring opportunities for doctoral cotutelle, and discussing joint projects in the areas of **Software Engineering**, **Artificial Intelligence**, and **Human-Computer Interaction**.

The agenda included meetings with research groups, institutional presentations of PPgSI, and a survey of international funding calls (DAAD, CAPES-DAAD PROBRAL).

Outcomes will include the arrival of visiting researchers and new mobility opportunities for students in the program.
