The Graduate Program in Information Systems has published **Edital EACH/CPG 107/2025** for the selection of candidates for the **doctoral program** with admission in 2026.

## Requirements

- Master's degree in Information Systems, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or related fields recognized by CAPES.
- Research project aligned with one of the program's two lines: *Systems Management and Development* or *Systems Intelligence*.
- Letter of acceptance from an advisor accredited in PPgSI.

## Documents

The full list of documents and the schedule are available in [Open calls](/admission/open) and on the program's official website.
