EACH-USP hosted a representative of the **Association for Information Systems (AIS)**, the main international society in the field, for an open talk. The presentation covered global research networks in Information Systems, publication opportunities in AIS journals and conferences, and ways for Brazilian researchers to enter the international circuit.

The event was open to PPgSI students, faculty members, and the external community, and included a question session and networking at the end.
