The Graduate Program in Information Systems (PPgSI) at EACH-USP has published **Edital EACH/CPG 90/2025** for admission to the master's program in the 1st semester of 2026.

## Deadlines

- **Applications**: by November 30, 2025
- **Written exam**: December 15, 2025
- **Interviews**: December 18–20, 2025
- **Final result**: January 10, 2026
