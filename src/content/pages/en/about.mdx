## Introduction

In the current context, computer systems are prevalent in all human activities. The economy and social relations are concentrated on computers and networked devices, transforming commerce, services, industrial production, education, entertainment and interpersonal relationships in the digital space.

Modern computer systems process large volumes of data in diverse formats (image, sound, multimedia, three-dimensional environments) to solve extremely complex problems. Examples include automated operations on stock exchanges and simulators for driver licensing.

## Sociotechnical context

These highly complex systems do not work in isolation; their behavior depends on their organizational positioning and on their interaction with users and other systems. The emergent behavior of this interaction is observed in areas such as natural resource management, energy systems control, disaster response and on-demand media consumption.

The professionals who develop and manage these systems need multidisciplinary knowledge: computer science, economics, management, business administration, psychology, health and sociology.

## The PPgSI program

The Graduate Program in Information Systems of the School of Arts, Sciences and Humanities of the University of São Paulo aims to contribute to solving complex problems through scientific research and the training of human resources.

PPgSI offers two academic programs:

- **Master's degree in Information Systems**
- **PhD in Information Systems**

## Area of concentration

The area of concentration is called **Computing Methodology and Techniques**; it proposes, develops, applies and evaluates methodologies and techniques in the main fields of computing to solve challenges in different application areas.

The program has a strong focus on applied research, aligned with the paradigm of innovative companies ("startups") that integrate diverse data to offer solutions to urban and social problems.

## Research lines

Within the area of concentration, two research lines are defined:

- **Systems management and development**: Covers aspects of the management and development of computer systems, including databases, software engineering, information technology management and human-computer interaction.
- **Systems intelligence**: Aims to promote the use and development of intelligent techniques, including artificial intelligence, graphics processing and pattern recognition.

## Application areas

PPgSI directs its research efforts to more than ten application areas:

- Corporate environments and business processes
- Bioinformatics
- Biometrics
- Economics
- Education and distance education
- E-government
- Internet and social networks
- Games
- Linguistics and natural language
- Robotics
- Health

These areas are dynamic, reflecting the interests of researchers, the needs of society and established partnerships.

## Alumni profile

The Master and Doctor of Science from PPgSI has "in-depth knowledge of computational methodologies and techniques, as well as of scientific methodology, capable of integrating concepts from the field of computing and from the application area."

Alumni are prepared to:

- Use, develop and evaluate computational methodologies and techniques
- Solve challenges of highly complex systems
- Transmit the knowledge developed
- Carry out innovation and technology transfer

## Skills and professional roles

Alumni have the technical and scientific competence to work in different types of roles:

- **Private, public or government organizations**: Strategic positions and roles that deal with the complexity of computer systems
- **Research and development centers**: Major national or multinational companies developing highly complex systems
- **Startups**: Young, entrepreneurial companies related to computing
- **Education**: Teaching courses in computer science and information systems at the undergraduate and graduate levels

## Basic procedures

Students must complete two activities: take courses and conduct research under the supervision of a faculty member accredited in PPgSI. The ideal time to completion is two years, with a maximum of three years.

Two required courses (8 credits each):

- Analysis of Algorithms and Data Structures
- Research Methodology in Information Systems

Students must take four courses in total (two required and two elective), preferably within one year.

## Additional requirements

- Demonstrate English language proficiency through a specific exam at the beginning of the program
- Pass the courses taken
- Present a master's thesis (master's program) or doctoral dissertation (doctoral program) approved in an oral defense before an examining committee

## Time commitment

Full-time dedication to the program is not required, although it is advisable. Only full-time students may apply for scholarships. Part-time students must meet the same requirements within the same time frame.

## Desired background

**Requirements for the master's program** — Undergraduate degree:

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Informatics or Systems Analysis (or equivalent bachelor's degrees specifically in computing)
- Teaching degree in Computing or Informatics (or equivalent teaching degrees specifically in computing)
- Technology degree in computing, with a workload reasonably close to that of bachelor's programs and broad coverage of basic and advanced computing courses
- Bachelor's degree in related fields (Engineering, Business Administration, Statistics, Physics, Mathematics), with a clear curricular emphasis on computing, including advanced algorithmic skills

**Requirements for the doctoral program**: Undergraduate and/or master's degree in the fields listed above for the master's program.
