## PPgSI regulations

### 2022 — current regulations

[NEW Regulations of the Graduate Program in Information Systems (PPgSI) of EACH-USP](https://leginf.usp.br/?resolucao=resolucao-copgr-no-8289-de-17-de-agosto-de-2022), dated 17/08/2022. Valid for students enrolled from 17/08/2022 onward, or before that date but who opted to switch to the new regulations.

**For program students:** the option for the NEW PPgSI Regulations, in accordance with the new Graduate Bylaws, may be exercised within 12 (twelve) months from 17/08/2022.

[Form to request placement under the NEW PPgSI Regulations](http://ppgsi.each.usp.br/arquivos/PPgSI-Solicitacao-Aluno-Enquadramento-Novo-Regimento-2022.docx)

### 2015

[OLD PPgSI Regulations](http://ppgsi.each.usp.br/arquivos/PPgSI-Regulamento-Aprovado-CoPGr-12Jun2015.pdf), dated 17/06/2015. Valid for students enrolled until 22/02/2019 who did not opt to switch to the new regulations.
