**Basic field of knowledge:** Computer Science

**Area of concentration:** Computing Methodology and Techniques

Research at PPgSI is organized into two research lines, each with specific research areas. These lines intersect with various application areas, forming orthogonal research axes that reflect the inherently applied and interdisciplinary nature of Information Systems.

## Systems management and development

Study, proposal, application and evaluation of computing methodologies and techniques in the context of developing Information Systems of strategic value for various application areas.

Research areas:

- Databases
- Software engineering
- Information technology management
- Human-computer interaction
- Computer networks

[See details](/about/research/gestao-e-desenvolvimento)

## Systems intelligence

Study, proposal, application and evaluation of computing methodologies and techniques in the development of advanced and innovative algorithmic solutions to problems in various application areas.

Research areas:

- Artificial intelligence
- Graphics processing
- Pattern recognition
- Sound and music computing

[See details](/about/research/inteligencia-de-sistemas)
