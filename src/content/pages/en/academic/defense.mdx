## Preliminary information

Check before thesis submission:

- [PPgSI regulations](http://ppgsi.each.usp.br/normas-e-regimentos/)
- CPG-EACH regulations
- USP graduate regulations

Submission is done digitally through the Janus system.

## Forms

- Master's program: Form to apply for a master's thesis defense
- Doctoral program: Form to apply for a doctoral dissertation defense

## General procedures

- Follow the BDTD-USP guidelines (ABNT style)
- Include the cataloging record
- Mention funding in the acknowledgments section
- Attach proof of submission/publication of scientific papers co-authored with the advisor
- Fill out the form on the "impact of the thesis or dissertation on society"
- Hybrid examining committees do not require special authorization; fully remote ones require a formal request

## Written evaluation (doctoral program)

Full examiners must carry out a detailed evaluation within 30 days of their appointment. Maximum interval between receipt of the reports and the defense: 45 days.

## Important information

- Committees are defined at regular meetings of the CCP and CPG
- There is no automatic provision for expenses with external examiners
- EACH provides for payment of an honorarium to external examiners

## Final version of the master's thesis/doctoral dissertation

No later than **60 days** after the defense is approved, send to the SPG by email at **atendimentopos-each@usp.br**:

### 1. Corrected version of the master's thesis

- PDF format
- Include the [cataloging record](https://www5.each.usp.br/biblioteca-ficha-catalografica/)
- Follow the [USP guidelines (ABNT)](https://teses.usp.br/index.php?option=com_content&view=article&id=52&Itemid=67&lang=pt-br)
- Mention funding in the acknowledgments
- Comply with Resolução [CoPGr No 6018](http://www.leginf.usp.br/?resolucao=resolucao-copgr-no-6018-de-13-de-outubro-de-2011)

### 2. BDTD registration notice

- Register the electronic version in the [USP BDTD](http://www.teses.usp.br/)
- Request a password from the SPG after the defense

### 3. Authorization form

- [Authorization form for publication in the BDTD](http://www5.each.usp.br/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/POS-DEF-TermoAutorizacao.docx)

### 4. Diploma request

- [Diploma request](http://ppgsi.each.usp.br/arquivos/PPgSI-Requerimento-Diploma.docx) with copies of RG, certificate and undergraduate diploma

### 5. Alumni registration

- Register in the [Sistema de Egressos/Alumni da USP](https://uspdigital.usp.br/egressosusp/)

### 6. Currículo Lattes update

- Proof of completion following the [standard](http://ppgsi.each.usp.br/arquivos/PPgSI-cadastro-do-mestrado-concluido-no-CV-Lattes.pdf)

### 7. Alumni spreadsheet

- Fill out the [PPgSI alumni spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1bYSjtxpqb8R0BwRJtIiF-fnvGy506MDmYHukDG0M8Wc/edit?usp=sharing)

See the [PPgSI regulations](http://ppgsi.each.usp.br/normas-e-regimentos/) (item XVII.3).
