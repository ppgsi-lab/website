### Form to request transfer from the master's program to the direct-entry doctorate

The transfer may be requested after passing the master's qualifying exam, at the suggestion of the majority of the examiners, within 60 days after the exam is approved. It must include: a new research project, a document from the committee recommending the transfer, and the academic transcript.

- [Transfer request form](http://ppgsi.each.usp.br/arquivos/PPgSI-solicitaçãoTransferênciaCurso.docx)

### Impact factors/indices (JCR/H-Index)

According to the current regulations, the factors considered in the analysis of publications are JCR and H-Index:

**For journals:** JCR or H-Index (whichever is better)

- [JCR](https://jcr.incites.thomsonreuters.com) (access via the USP network or VPN)
- [H-Index](http://www.scimagojr.com/journalsearch.php) from SCImago-Scopus

**For conferences:** [Google Scholar H5-Index](https://scholar.google.com/citations?view_op=top_venues&hl=en)
