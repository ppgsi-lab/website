## Scholarship rules

### General information

<p class="note">"There is no guarantee of scholarships for all incoming regular students in the PPgSI master's and doctoral programs." Availability depends on several factors, such as the time of admission, the number of available scholarships and the situation of current scholarship holders. Expressing interest in a scholarship and being ranked on the list does not guarantee that one will be awarded.</p>

### Capes / Social Demand (DS) scholarships

Students interested in a Capes/DS scholarship from the PPgSI quota must express their interest at their first enrollment, on the enrollment form. If the interest arises later, inform the program office by email.

**Important requirements:** See [Portaria no 76 de 14 de Abril de 2010](http://cad.capes.gov.br/ato-administrativo-detalhar?idAtoAdmElastic=741#anchor), which establishes that doctoral scholarship holders must complete a teaching internship.

### Capes scholarships (program and PRPG quotas)

See the [PAE](/academic/pae) program.

### Fapesp scholarships

The student and the advisor must apply directly to the agency with a specific research project. Official enrollment is required, although the project may be prepared beforehand.

See the [Fapesp website](http://www.fapesp.br/bolsas/).

### Alternative internships

Master's students may do internships in companies. Finding a company is the student's responsibility. Once selected, contact the SPG to formalize the internship. The internship must be in computing, information systems, information technology or related fields.

## PROAP funding

### Application instructions

Applicants must:

1. Follow the guidelines for requesting PROAP funding on the CPG-EACH page and access the specific form provided there
2. Fill out the PROAP-AUXPE form provided
3. Send the completed document to **ppgsi-each@usp.br**
4. Wait for instructions from the finance department before incurring any expenses

### Important information

Payments are made by the applicant, and reimbursements are made after submission of receipts validated by the finance department.

Expenses include airfare, registration fees and similar items, which must be authorized in advance.
