The Teaching Improvement Program (PAE) aims to improve the training of graduate students in teaching activities at the undergraduate level.

## Program structure

PAE consists of two stages:

1. Take a pedagogical preparation course (the course **EAH5001 — Pedagogical Preparation**, available in the EACH general course list, is recommended)
2. Supervised teaching internship ("PAE teaching internship")

<p class="note"><strong>Capes scholarship holders</strong> must take part in the PAE program before the master's thesis defense.</p>

## Additional resources

For more details on the program guidelines, related ordinances, the selection process for the PAE teaching internship and information about the responsible committee, see the [page dedicated to PAE](http://www5.each.usp.br/pae).
