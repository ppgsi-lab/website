## Required scores

The minimum scores in the English and Portuguese proficiency exams depend on the regulations the student is bound by.

### Regulations of December 17, 2013

[Deliberação CCP-PPgSI-2-2015](http://ppgsi.each.usp.br/arquivos/PPgSI-Deliberacao-Proficiencias-2_2015.pdf)<span class="ft">PDF</span>, of November 4, 2015, defines the minimum scores in English and Portuguese.

### 2019 regulations

For students under the regulations of [Resolução CoPGr 7609](http://www.leginf.usp.br/?resolucao=resolucao-copgr-no-7609-de-21-de-fevereiro-de-2019), of February 21, 2019, the English scores are set out in items V.1.4 and V.1.5.

### Submission of proof

Proof of proficiency is sent by email to the [SPG](http://ppgsi.each.usp.br/contato).

## FFLCH-USP Language Center exams

## English exams from União Cultural Brasil-Estados Unidos

União Cultural administers exams by field. Ask for the computer science exam or, if there is none, the exact sciences exam, and tell the examiner the field of your program.
