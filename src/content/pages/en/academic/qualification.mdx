## Examining committee composition

### Master's program

3 members holding doctoral degrees. The advisor/co-advisor may chair the committee with voting rights.

### Doctoral program

3 voting members (holding doctoral degrees, including at least one from outside the program) + a non-voting chair. The advisor/co-advisor serves exclusively as chair.

The CCP defines the committees at its regular meetings. In January and July, the program coordination appoints them and ratifies them later.

## Important information

- Master's theses must follow the BDTD-USP guidelines, preferably ABNT
- The coordination does not provide for expenses with external examiners
- No honorarium is paid to examiners
- Proap funds may be available (contact the coordinator)
- For videoconferencing, request a room reservation from the SPG

See the [PPgSI regulations](http://ppgsi.each.usp.br/normas-e-regimentos/) (item VIII).
