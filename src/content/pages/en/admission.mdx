Complete information on application and enrollment in the PPgSI master's and doctoral programs.

## Master's program

### Application to the selection process for regular students

Filling out **both parts of the application form** is mandatory. Filling out only one of the parts will result in the application not being accepted. When you fill out each part of the form, you will receive an automatic email confirming that it was filled out. Fill out the forms only during the application period (May 1–30, 2025).

- [Regular student application form for the master's program (Part 1/2)](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdhjySbCi8tY9GaVmPDDitFPbknqHqwxd_SWsIlvCCtJxAGkA/viewform)
- [Regular student application form for the master's program (Part 2/2)](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdb8W5g__pM_svf07tQX_PjKipVAjYkeB3HIVeZ1hlbK0mPGQ/viewform)

All documents related to the application to the selection process for regular master's students must be sent electronically to: **inscricaoppgsi-each \[in the usp.br domain\]**

**Attention:** questions should not be sent to the email address above, but to the [appropriate addresses](http://ppgsi.each.usp.br/contato/).

### Required documents (master's program)

1. Undergraduate academic transcript (plain copy attached as PDF)
2. Currículo Lattes (plain copy attached as PDF), produced on the [Lattes platform](http://lattes.cnpq.br), updated with all academic and professional information the applicant considers relevant
3. Statement of research intent of at most 4 (four) pages for each research project chosen, as defined in the call. It must be submitted as attached files (PDF format) and the document may not exceed 4 pages. The document must clearly state which research project the statement of research intent is for, using the same project code and name. The project must contain items such as: context and motivation, objectives and proposal, research method, expected results, general schedule.

### Optional documents (master's program)

1. Proof of having taken the "National Exam for Admission to Graduate Programs in Computing (Poscomp)" (plain copy attached as PDF), administered by the [Brazilian Computer Society (SBC)](http://www.sbc.org.br), with the score obtained
2. Proof of passing the course "Analysis of Algorithms and Data Structures (SIN5013)" offered by PPgSI (plain copy attached as PDF), with the grade obtained
3. Academic transcripts from graduate programs (plain copy attached as PDF)

### Instructions for submitting documents (master's program)

1. Use **exactly** the following naming pattern for the PDF files sent (where XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX represents the CandidateFullName):
   - `HistoricoGraduacao-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX.pdf`
   - `CVLattes-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX.pdf`
   - `PoscompYYYY-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX.pdf` (where YYYY = year in which the Poscomp was taken)
   - `SIN5013-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX.pdf`
   - `HistoricoPosGraduacao-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX.pdf`
2. All documents must be submitted exclusively as PDF files (that is, do not send DOC, JPEG, etc. files)
3. Each document must be in a separate PDF file (that is, do not send everything grouped in a single PDF file)
4. Do not split a single document into several separate PDF files
5. Group everything in a single compressed file (preferably in ZIP format)

**Note:** Recommendation letters are no longer required for admission to the master's program.

## Important notes on enrollment (master's and doctoral programs)

For applicants approved in the selection process:

- Incoming students must contact their respective advisors for help in choosing the courses to take (the enrollment form must be signed by the advisor before the student submits it; the signature may be digital)
- Incoming students must pay attention to the required documents. For admission to the master's program, incoming students must pay particular attention to the degree conferral date if they are recent graduates; the student must already have proof of degree conferral for enrollment in the graduate program to be completed
- All incoming students are strongly advised to read the Program Regulations carefully, as well as the other higher-level bylaws (CPG and PRPG). See: [Regulations and bylaws](http://ppgsi.each.usp.br/normas-e-regimentos/)

### Doctoral program — 2024 (rolling admissions)

- **Application period:** 19/01/2024 - 30/11/2024 (rolling admissions)
- **Announcement of accepted applications:** Within 30 calendar days after application
- **Appeal deadline:** Within 3 calendar days after the announcement of accepted applications
- **Appeal results:** Within 6 calendar days after the announcement of accepted applications
- **Announcement of approved applicants:** Within 45 calendar days after the application is accepted
- **Confirmation of interest:** Within 30 days after the announcement of the result (send an email to ppgsi-each@usp.br)
- **Enrollment:** Within 180 calendar days after the result is approved

**Note:** The Poscomp exam is not mandatory for the selection process, but it is recommended, since it is part of the scoring criteria for ranking and elimination. Check the specific call for full details.
