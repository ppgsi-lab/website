## Scholarship programs outside PPgSI

International applicants who want an acceptance letter to apply for a scholarship (PEC-PG or funding agencies) must submit the documents listed below.

### Required documents

1. Student application form (Word)
2. Two recommendation letters (sent directly by the recommenders)
3. Copy of academic transcripts (undergraduate and graduate)
4. Curriculum vitae with photo (Plataforma Lattes)
5. Research project proposals (optional)
6. Information on the type of scholarship and the funding agency/body

### Procedure

- Documents in PDF to **ppgsi-each@usp.br**
- Stating that it is an international application
- **At least 21 days before** the scholarship deadline

### Guidance

- Check the list of advisors to verify alignment
- Contacting advisors in advance is recommended
- Once the request is approved, an acceptance letter is issued, conditional on obtaining the scholarship

## Scholarships within PPgSI or master's program without a scholarship

International applicants seeking a Brazilian scholarship or taking the master's program without a scholarship must take part in the **regular selection process** for regular students.

See the [selection process](/admission) page for more information on application and required documents.
