## What special students are

Special students take only individual courses, with no formal link to the program. They must meet the same requirements as regular students (minimum attendance and assessments).

The credits may be counted in an eventual transition to regular student, subject to the advisor's evaluation. Passing the courses **does not guarantee later admission** as a regular student.

They must have completed an undergraduate degree, except for USP undergraduate students. An enrollment fee is charged per course (USP undergraduate students are exempt).

## Application to the selection process

Applications are made on the general EACH-USP graduate website. The faculty members of each course evaluate the requests. Each course has a variable number of places for special students.

**Special requirement for SIN5000:** during the application period, a document from a PPgSI advisor confirming ongoing research must be sent to ppgsi-each@usp.br.

## Recommended courses

- **SIN5013 – Analysis of Algorithms and Data Structures** (required, priority)
- **SIN5000 – Research Methodology in IS** (only for applicants with research in collaboration with a PPgSI faculty member)

## Notes

- Taking individual courses does not constitute enrollment in the master's program
- Record them in the CV Lattes only as "formação complementar"
- See Section IX of the USP Graduate Bylaws

## Calendar

Applications for special students are usually open twice a year:

- **January:** for first-semester courses (classes start in February)
- **July:** for second-semester courses (classes start in August)

<p class="note"><strong>Important notice:</strong> The exact period to apply as a special student is usually announced only in the month before it opens. Follow the EACH-USP graduate website for updated dates and periods.</p>
