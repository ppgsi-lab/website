## About the program

### 1) What does PPgSI stand for?

It is the acronym for "Programa de Pós-graduação em Sistemas de Informação" (Graduate Program in Information Systems).

### 2) What programs does PPgSI offer?

PPgSI offers an academic master's program and an academic doctoral program.

### 3) Are the PPgSI master's and doctoral programs recognized?

Yes, PPgSI is recommended by Capes (Coordenação de Aperfeiçoamento de Pessoal de Nível Superior).

### 4) What is Capes?

Capes is the Ministry of Education agency responsible for evaluating and recommending graduate programs in Brazil.

### 5) Since when have the PPgSI programs been offered?

The master's program has been offered since 2010 and the doctoral program since 2019.

### 6) What degrees does PPgSI award?

Master of Science and Doctor of Science, both earned in the Information Systems program.

## Differences between programs

### 7) What is the difference between an academic master's and a professional master's, and between an academic doctorate and a professional doctorate?

The programs offered by PPgSI are academic. For the exact differences, see the Capes website.

### 8) What is the difference between a master's program and a specialization program or an MBA?

The PPgSI master's program is *stricto sensu*, whereas specialization programs and MBAs are *lato sensu*.

### 9) What are the differences between the academic master's and the academic doctoral programs?

Both train professionals for scientific research, but the doctorate is a more advanced stage. The master's program ideally lasts 2 years, the doctoral program 4 years. The scientific contribution expected from a doctoral student is greater and more original.

### 10) What is a direct-entry doctorate?

The possibility of pursuing a doctorate without having completed a master's program, in exceptional cases when the student demonstrates a strong capacity for research.

### 11) What forms of direct-entry doctorate are there at PPgSI?

Two forms: applying directly for the direct-entry doctorate with a letter from an advisor, or transferring from the master's to the doctoral program during the qualifying exam.

## Academic applicability

### 12) Are academic master's and doctoral programs only for those who want to work in academia?

No. What is learned is useful for specialized work in many types of organizations.

### 13) What is the difference between graduate programs in "Information Systems", "Computer Science", "Informatics", "Applied Computing", etc.?

All are governed by the same Capes Computer Science area. The differences lie in the focus and type of research of the advisors.

## Technical requirements

### 14) Do I need to know how to program to take the PPgSI master's or doctoral program in Information Systems?

Yes, and well. The programs assume advanced knowledge of software programming.

### 15) Which programming languages do I need to master to take the master's or doctoral program in Information Systems?

No specific language is required. It depends on the advisor and the research project.

### 16) What should I know about algorithms?

The required course "Analysis of Algorithms and Data Structures" covers the theory. Suggested reading: "Algoritmos — Teoria e Prática" by Cormen et al.

## Program structure

### 17) What is the main area of PPgSI? What is an area of concentration?

PPgSI works in the Computer Science area, with a concentration in "Computational Methodology and Techniques".

### 18) Is PPgSI organized into research lines or research areas?

Yes, two lines: "Systems Management and Development" and "Systems Intelligence", divided into seven areas.

### 19) What are application areas?

The application of computational methodologies to solve problems in other fields, such as chemistry, education, fashion and health.

### 20) What is expected of a student in an academic master's or doctoral program such as PPgSI's?

The student is expected to want to prepare for a scientific and technological career, with a strong capacity for abstraction and systematization.

### 21) What results are expected from the student who completes a master's or doctoral program such as those offered by PPgSI?

The student is expected to produce scientific papers in relevant national and international venues, coauthored with their advisors.

## Time commitment and format

### 22) Is exclusive or full-time dedication required? Or is it possible to take the PPgSI master's and doctoral programs part-time?

It is not required, but the student needs to set aside considerable time at every stage.

### 23) What are the stages or activities of the PPgSI master's and doctoral programs?

Two main stages: (1) taking courses and (2) a research project with a qualifying exam and a defense.

### 24) How are master's and doctoral students assessed at PPgSI?

Passing courses, an English proficiency exam, the qualifying exam, the master's thesis/doctoral dissertation defense, semiannual reports and the production of scientific papers.

## Courses

### 25) Are PPgSI courses different for the master's and doctoral programs?

No, there are graduate courses that count for both the master's and the doctoral program.

### 26) Which set of courses must be taken in the PPgSI master's and doctoral programs?

Four courses: two required and two elective. Doctoral students who did their master's at PPgSI take four electives.

### 27) Which required and elective courses are offered next semester?

See: [ppgsi.each.usp.br/semestre-atual-e-proximos](http://ppgsi.each.usp.br/semestre-atual-e-proximos)

### 28) Are any courses scheduled in the evening for the PPgSI master's and doctoral programs?

Usually not. Occasionally a faculty member may ask to offer one, but this is quite rare.

### 29) How long does each course last?

Four class hours per week (except one, with two) over periods of 15 teaching weeks (March–June or August–November).

### 30) What happens if the student fails courses or cannot complete all the required courses?

A student who fails the same course twice, or three different courses, is automatically dismissed. The same applies if they do not pass within the deadline.

### 31) Can courses already taken in other graduate programs be credited?

Yes, provided they come from in-person *stricto sensu* programs that were not completed. Each request is assessed individually.

### 32) Can courses already taken in undergraduate programs be credited?

No, only courses from in-person *stricto sensu* graduate programs.

### 33) Can courses already taken in distance-learning graduate programs be credited?

No, only from in-person *stricto sensu* programs.

### 34) Can courses already taken in graduate programs that were completed be credited?

No, only courses from programs that were not completed. If you have already completed a program, its courses cannot be reused.

### 35) Is it possible to take courses in other USP graduate programs?

Yes, normally, as if they were offered by PPgSI. Restriction: the faculty member must accept.

## Qualifying exam and defense

### 36) What is the qualifying exam in the PPgSI master's and doctoral programs?

An exam that checks whether the research project is on the right track, held approximately midway through its development.

### 37) What happens if the student fails the qualifying exam or cannot take it within the required period?

A student who fails it a second time is automatically dismissed. A student who does not take it within the deadline is also dismissed.

### 38) What happens if the student fails the master's thesis or doctoral dissertation defense?

A student who fails is dismissed without obtaining the degree and cannot try a second time. A student who does not hold it within the deadline is also dismissed.

## Deadlines

### 39) How long should I take to complete my master's or doctoral program at PPgSI?

Master's: ideally 24 months, exceptionally up to 30 months. Doctorate: ideally 48 months, up to 54 months.

### 40) Can I complete my master's program at PPgSI in less than 24 months, or my doctoral program in less than 48 months?

Yes, if you took courses as a special student. In the best case: master's in 12 months, doctorate in 36 months.

### 41) How long should I take to complete my courses? And my research project?

Courses: two semesters, exceptionally three. Project: at most 12 months for the master's, 36 for the doctorate.

### 42) Can I take courses and work on my research project in parallel?

Yes, with care. First two semesters: emphasis on courses, but with initial work on the project.

## Language

### 43) Do I need an English proficiency certificate (for example, TOEFL) to take the program?

It is not required at entry, but the student has a little over one year to demonstrate proficiency.

## Advisors

### 44) How can I choose an advisor for my master's or doctoral program at PPgSI?

The advisor is defined after passing the selection process. Contacting potential advisors before/during the selection process is recommended.

### 45) Can I talk in person with a faculty member I would like to have as my advisor?

It depends on their availability. Initial contact by email is suggested.

### 46) How can I find more information about a faculty member I would like to have as my advisor?

See their Currículo Lattes, available on the advisors page. See their personal page and published papers.

### 47) How do I choose my research project? Do I choose the topic?

Generally, the topic is defined by the advisor based on their ongoing projects.

### 48) Can I develop an information system as the topic of my master's research project? And for the doctorate?

In general, no. The focus must be research, not development. Systems are supporting methodological steps, not the main result.

### 49) Is the research intention I filled in on the application form not taken into account when defining my research project?

For the master's program, the research intention assesses the ability to understand research. For the doctoral program, a conversation with the advisor in advance is expected.

### 50) Can I change advisors during my master's or doctoral program at PPgSI, if I wish?

It is possible, but not desirable. Exceptional cases are covered by the regulations.

### 51) How can I get a clearer idea of what a research project and a master's thesis or doctoral dissertation are?

See master's theses and doctoral dissertations already defended: [ppgsi.each.usp.br/dissertacoes-defendidas](http://ppgsi.each.usp.br/dissertacoes-defendidas)

### 52) Can I have a co-advisor for my master's or doctoral program at PPgSI?

Yes, after enrollment. Specific rules must be followed.

## Nature of the research

### 53) Does the research project need to be in the Computing area?

Yes, it must be in the Computer Science area, although interdisciplinary projects are possible.

### 54) Does the research project need to be theoretical or applied research?

The main focus is applied research, but theoretical research is also welcome. It depends on the advisor's profile.

### 55) Will my master's thesis or doctoral dissertation be the main product of my program at PPgSI?

No. The main product is the publication of scientific papers in relevant venues.

### 56) Does the quality of the papers produced matter?

Yes, it is very important. Preference is given to international coverage.

### 57) Are patents and software production equivalent to papers?

Patents and software registrations are desirable, but they do not replace the publication of quality scientific papers.

## Admission requirements

### 58) Do I need a degree in Computer Science or Information Systems to take the master's program at PPgSI?

It is not required, but highly desirable. Accepted: Computer Engineering, Systems Analysis, etc. Also related programs with an emphasis on computing.

### 59) Do I need a master's degree in Computer Science or Information Systems to take the doctoral program at PPgSI?

No, but it is highly desirable. Accepted: Informatics, Applied Computing, Computer Engineering, and related fields that overlap with computing.

### 60) Do I need to prove my level of knowledge in Computing to apply to the selection process?

Not necessarily. The academic transcript and Lattes already indicate it. Optionally: Poscomp or the course SIN5013 as a special student.

### 61) Which editions of Poscomp are valid for the selection process?

Master's: the last two years. Doctorate: the last three years.

### 62) Is there an equivalent to the Poscomp exam to prove my level of knowledge?

Yes, the course SIN5013 (Analysis of Algorithms and Data Structures) taken as a special student.

### 63) If I have neither taken the Poscomp exam nor the course SIN5013, do I still have a chance of being accepted?

Yes, there are chances. Proof of knowledge is optional. However, there are no guarantees.

## Documentation

### 64) Can I submit a curriculum vitae in my own format, other than the Plataforma Lattes format?

No. Only Lattes CVs are accepted.

### 65) Is it enough to fill in my Currículo Lattes on the online platform, or do I need to generate a PDF?

You must generate a PDF version.

### 66) What should I include in my Currículo Lattes to take part in the selection process?

Include all important information. The academic community expects detail, not lean CVs.

### 67) What happens if the people I asked for recommendation letters do not send them?

You are not disqualified. You will not score points for that item.

### 68) Whom should I ask for recommendation letters?

Faculty members and researchers. For the doctoral program, ideally one from your master's advisor.

### 69) Can I attend classes as an auditing student?

The regulations make no provision for auditing students.

## Selection process

### 70) When is the selection process for admission as a regular student to the PPgSI master's program? And to the doctoral program?

Master's: applications in October–November (results in December, start in March) and April–May (results in June, start in August). Doctorate: every semester or on a rolling basis.

### 71) How many openings are there in the selection process?

There are no predefined numbers. It depends on the accredited advisors, the students they already advise, etc.

### 72) What kind of undergraduate degree is required to apply for an opening in the master's program?

Almost every kind is accepted. Not accepted: short-cycle teaching degrees (licenciaturas curtas) and sequential programs (cursos sequenciais).

### 73) Are all kinds of undergraduate degrees evaluated the same way in the master's selection process?

No, they may be evaluated with different weights.

### 74) What kind of master's degree is required to apply for an opening in the doctoral program?

Only academic master's degrees. The direct-entry doctorate is an alternative.

### 75) Is there an application fee?

No, the application is free.

### 76) Do I need proof of graduation to apply to the master's selection process?

Not to take part in the selection process, but it will be required for enrollment.

### 77) Do I need proof of my master's thesis defense to apply to the doctoral selection process?

Not to take part in the selection process, but it will be required for enrollment.

## Costs and funding

### 78) What is the monthly tuition for the PPgSI master's and doctoral programs?

The programs are free, as are all academic programs at public institutions such as USP.

### 79) Besides not having to pay, can I also get a scholarship?

Yes, there are Capes scholarships and the possibility of submitting requests to Fapesp. Exclusive dedication is required.

### 80) How much is the scholarship?

It depends on the period and the funding agency. See: [Scholarships and PROAP](/academic/funding)

### 81) Are scholarships available for all interested students with exclusive dedication?

Not necessarily. A waiting list is formed, with no guarantee for everyone.

### 82) What should I do to get my scholarship?

Indicate your interest when applying to the selection process. When enrolling as a regular student, state your interest. The coordination ranks the interested students.

### 83) What is the procedure for foreign applicants interested in the PPgSI master's and doctoral programs?

It depends on the type of scholarship. See: [International students](/admission/international)

### 84) Are there other forms of funding besides these scholarships?

Yes, other scholarships, such as the PAE teaching internship, may be offered. See: [PAE teaching internship](/academic/pae)

## Complementary activities

### 85) Is it possible to do an internship during the PPgSI master's and doctoral programs?

Yes, students may do internships at companies. Finding the companies is the student's responsibility.

### 86) Is accommodation (student housing) available for students?

Information about CRUSP: [COSEAS website](http://www.usp.br/coseas/COSEASHP/COSEAS2010.html)

### 87) Is there a USP office that can help students coming from other cities?

Yes, the Social Service (SAS). See: the SAS EACH-USP page or the central USP SAS.

### 88) Is it necessary to live in São Paulo to be a regular student?

Not necessarily, as long as you are available for weekly classes and meetings. Scholarship holders are required to.

### 89) Can the PPgSI master's and doctoral programs be taken by distance learning?

No, the programs are in person. All courses and activities are in person.

### 90) What is the minimum attendance required to pass courses?

75%, in accordance with MEC regulations.

## Special students

### 91) What is a special student?

A student who takes only individual courses, with no regular affiliation with the program or an advisor.

### 92) How are special students selected?

Each faculty member uses their own selection criteria. The coordination approves or rejects the faculty member's recommendation.

### 93) How many openings are there for special students?

The number depends on the faculty member teaching the course. Deciding is the faculty member's prerogative.

### 94) Is there a waiting list in the special student selection process?

There is no waiting list for special students.

### 95) If there are openings, will all special student applicants be selected?

Not necessarily. The faculty member assesses whether the applicant has the minimum background.

### 96) Is it necessary to start as a special student? Or is it possible to start directly as a regular student?

You can start as a special student or directly as a regular student.

### 97) Is it possible to take PPgSI courses as a special student?

Yes, provided the faculty member in charge and the coordination approve.

### 98) Can courses taken as a special student be credited if I enroll as a regular student?

Yes, subject to specific rules and the advisor's agreement.

### 99) What is the time limit for crediting a course taken as a special student?

At most 36 months before the initial enrollment as a regular student.

### 100) Does a special student have no advisor?

Correct, only regular students have advisors.

### 101) Which courses are most recommended to take as a special student?

Mainly "Analysis of Algorithms and Data Structures" and elective courses related to the area of interest.

### 102) Which course is not recommended to take as a special student?

"Research Methodology in Information Systems" should not be taken as a special student.

### 103) Is it certain that I will be admitted as a regular student after taking courses as a special student?

No. Passing courses does not guarantee future admission as a regular student.

## Location

### 104) Where is PPgSI located?

At EACH (School of Arts, Sciences and Humanities) of USP, on the east campus in São Paulo. Address: Rua Arlindo Béttio, 1000, Ermelino Matarazzo, 03828-000, São Paulo, SP, Brazil.
